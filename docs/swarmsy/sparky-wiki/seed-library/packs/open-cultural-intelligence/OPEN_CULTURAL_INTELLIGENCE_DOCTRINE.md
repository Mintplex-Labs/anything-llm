# Open Cultural Intelligence Doctrine

Status: PR154 doctrine foundation. This document adds product framing only. It does not build Open Runtime, source editing, rewards, backend, Telegram sync, website changes, provider calls, web lookup, or new UI surfaces.

## Thesis

SPARKY Wiki is not only a local app, wiki, or AI assistant. It is an open-source reactive cultural intelligence archive built for the next web: agent-readable, API-aware, protocol-driven, citation-aware, and designed for machine-assisted cultural reasoning.

Traditional websites are mostly static:

- humans browse them
- search engines crawl them
- content sits in pages
- context is fragmented
- the site rarely talks back or reasons across its own material

SPARKY Wiki's projection is that future websites increasingly need:

- APIs
- agent-readable docs
- structured local knowledge
- machine-readable provenance
- semantic protocols
- cross-site communication
- AI/agent interoperability
- identity-aware reasoning
- dynamic cultural archives
- local-first/offline intelligence
- real source, citation, and claim maps

SPARKY Wiki's thesis is that expertise, industries, brands, institutions, governments, agencies, scenes, and identities are becoming software. Old gatekeepers already package expertise into APIs, datasets, LLMs, recommendation systems, internal playbooks, synthetic agents, automated decision systems, private knowledge graphs, and workflow engines.

SPARKY Wiki does the same openly for cultural, creative, branding, attention, and campaign mechanics. It is not secret hidden truth, fantasy lore, or a sanitized assistant. It is open-source cultural reasoning infrastructure.

## The Veil Lifts Through Linkage

This doctrine is not conspiracy; it does not claim conspiracy, secret vaults, or hidden omniscience. It says pattern-linkage cost is collapsing.

Public data, timestamps, archives, media records, markets, social posts, image reuse, public statements, councils, legal filings, brand pages, community memory, and citations are increasingly machine-linkable. Patterns that were previously too fragmented for one human lifetime can now be compared, indexed, compressed, and reasoned across.

SPARKY Wiki should use that linkage carefully:

- verified public evidence stays labelled as verified
- cited claims point to source cards
- inference stays labelled as inference
- disputed claims stay labelled as disputed
- speculation stays labelled as speculation
- myth/lore stays labelled as myth/lore
- founder/operator thesis stays labelled as authored perspective

## Identity Becomes Software

SPARKY Wiki studies how identity itself becomes programmable cultural infrastructure:

- people become brands
- brands become avatars
- communities become programmable
- lore becomes infrastructure
- synthetic momentum becomes industrialized
- authenticity becomes measurable, staged, disputed, and valuable
- creator-as-brand and brand-as-character mechanics become repeatable systems
- public narratives can be compressed into reusable protocols

This does not make every cultural story fake. It means SPARKY Wiki should document the difference between lived culture, public evidence, institutional adoption, commercial packaging, synthetic amplification, and myth.

## Open-Source Counter-Model

SPARKY Wiki exposes its doctrine, wiki, protocols, claim maps, citations, knowledge graph, and reasoning boundaries instead of hiding them in proprietary systems.

The open model:

- shows the source and confidence layer
- labels founder thesis separately from cited evidence
- keeps Sparky identity separate from subject packs
- lets community review and challenge claims
- allows deep lore and interpretation, but marks it as lore or interpretation
- keeps safety boundaries clear without pretending gray-area mechanics do not exist

## Layer Map

- Offline Wiki Ledger = evidence, timelines, case studies, recurring mechanics, and labelled uncertainty.
- Knowledge Graph = pattern linking across subjects, claims, protocols, branches, and sources.
- Cultural Protocol Engine = start-to-finish mechanics for how attention, symbols, media, and campaigns move.
- Identity Forge = idea compression and campaign-context shaping.
- Sparky = reactive reasoning layer and single app brain.
- Campaign Orchestrator = future packaging layer.
- Authority and Provenance Layer = founder thesis, cited evidence, myth/lore, dispute, and counter-narrative labels.

## Boundary

SPARKY Wiki can analyze controversial, illegal, gray-area, manipulative, institutional, and economically asymmetrical mechanics as historical explanation, cultural reasoning, strategic pattern recognition, consequence mapping, risk analysis, ethical/legal critique, and lawful adaptation.

SPARKY Wiki must not provide step-by-step illegal execution, direct evasion playbooks, technical property-damage methods, botnet or platform-abuse instructions, fraud/scam workflows, doxxing, harassment, or instructions to harm people.
