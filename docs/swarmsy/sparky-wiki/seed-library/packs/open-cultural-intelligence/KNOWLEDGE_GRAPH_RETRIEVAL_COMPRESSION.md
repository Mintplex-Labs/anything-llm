# Knowledge Graph and Retrieval Compression

Status: local foundation. This is not Open Runtime, a source editor, a provider integration, or a web lookup system.

## Purpose

The Offline Wiki Ledger can grow into many markdown files. Sparky should not dump whole packs into chat. This foundation compresses existing subject packs into operational records:

- subject summary
- top concepts
- reusable frameworks
- strategy lessons
- source-backed claims
- canonical questions
- missing-data warnings
- freshness warnings
- relationship edges between packs
- compressed cultural protocol records
- compressed depth-layer records
- authority/provenance labels for founder thesis, cited evidence, myth/lore, dispute, public narrative, and needs-source material

SPARKY Wiki is a reactive cultural intelligence archive. The Knowledge Graph is the pattern-linking layer for that archive: it connects historical wiki material, strategic pattern ledgers, campaign analysis, symbolic intelligence, creative reasoning, and "how the game is played" explanations.

It does not claim secret hidden truth. It links observable recurring mechanics across culture, branding, media, movements, virality, propaganda, street art, campaigns, and attention systems.

It may link controversial and gray-area mechanics when they are historically, culturally, strategically, or economically relevant: vandalism as a historical/cultural mechanic, illegal placement as an attention mechanic, enforcement asymmetry, cleanup economics, council tolerance, controlled rebellion, fake authenticity, hidden sponsorship, synthetic momentum, coordinated amplification, and auction/value signaling. Those links are analysis and risk/consequence mapping, not operational playbooks.

Wiki Formula / Citation Tree records add subject root nodes, branch nodes, depth-layer nodes, contradiction/source-conflict nodes, claim nodes, source nodes, protocol nodes, related-claim links, disputed-claim markers, and freshness-risk markers. These records let Sparky answer from root summaries first, expand into branches for deep questions, and mention when a local wiki claim is source-backed, disputed, needs-source, myth/lore, or interpretation.

Banksy is a high-signal case study, not the whole point. The graph should also support patterns from Supreme, Red Bull, Nike, Apple, Bernays, ARGs, meme culture, underground scenes, political branding, and internet virality.

Layer map:

- Offline Wiki Ledger = evidence, timelines, case studies, and recurring mechanics.
- Knowledge Graph = pattern linking.
- Identity Forge = idea compression and campaign-context shaping.
- Multi-Layer Conversational Depth Engine = subject branch-layer pivots for contradiction, criticism, emotional interpretation, institutional response, source conflicts, mythology/lore, and modern equivalents.
- Sparky = reactive reasoning layer.
- Campaign Orchestrator = future packaging layer.

## Retrieval Priority

SPARKY Wiki retrieval must stay in this order:

1. Sparky core brain/app truth.
2. Current workspace/user documents.
3. Compressed Knowledge Graph records, including Offline Wiki Ledger compression, Cultural Protocol Engine records, and Multi-Layer Conversational Depth Engine records.
4. Full Offline Wiki Ledger / knowledge pack / protocol / depth branch / authority-provenance docs.
5. Live provider or web lookup when connected and needed.

Wiki packs never override Sparky persona, current app truth, provider routing, runtime action rules, or user workspace state.

## Graph Model

Node types:

- `subject`
- `branch`
- `depth_layer`
- `contradiction`
- `source_conflict`
- `concept`
- `tactic`
- `framework`
- `source`
- `claim`
- `question`
- `protocol`
- future `authority_label`

Protocol records are stored as compressed records with `subjectId` values such as `protocol.banksy_style_public_signal`, `protocol.controlled_rebellion_regeneration`, and `protocol.synthetic_momentum_manufactured_authenticity`. They are searchable local reference records, not root app-brain instructions.

Depth records are stored as compressed records with `subjectId` values such as `depth.graffiti.banksy.economics`, `depth.graffiti.banksy.criticism`, and `depth.graffiti.banksy.source-conflicts`. They are conversation pivots and graph records, not root app-brain instructions.

## Readiness And Confidence

Statuses:

- `ready`
- `usable`
- `expanded_draft`
- `starter`
- `needs_refresh`

Sparky should prefer higher readiness packs for direct answers. Lower readiness packs can still support partial answers, but Sparky must include missing-data/freshness warnings.

## Boundaries

- No AI generation is required to build compressed records.
- No provider/web/network call is required.
- No rewards/backend/Telegram sync/website changes.
- No source editor or Open Runtime.
- Gray-area and illegal-adjacent mechanics may be analyzed as history, culture, economics, enforcement asymmetry, risk, and lawful adaptation.
- No step-by-step criminal execution, direct evasion playbooks, technical property-damage methods, botnet/platform-abuse instructions, fraud/scam workflows, doxxing/harassment workflows, or instructions to harm people.
- No identity claims without evidence.
- Founder thesis, myth/lore, public narrative, counter-narrative, and needs-source material must stay labelled and must not override cited evidence or Sparky app truth.
