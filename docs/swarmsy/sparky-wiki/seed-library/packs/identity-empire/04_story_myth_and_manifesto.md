# Story, Myth, and Manifesto

## Purpose

Build the story engine. Raw, not corporate. People do not join bullet points. They join pressure, proof, myth, and a name that feels like it is moving.

## Sparky should build

- Origin story: where the signal started.
- Founder story: why this person can carry the mission.
- Myth: the larger symbolic frame.
- Manifesto: what the brand declares, rejects, and demands.
- Rebellion angle: what it refuses.
- Culture angle: what scene, taste, ritual, or code it represents.
- Local scene angle: why this place matters.
- Why now: why the timing is urgent.
- Why me: why the founder/user has the right to speak.
- Emotional stakes: what happens if nobody acts.
- Proof of struggle.
- Proof of transformation.

## Story formula

I saw `[problem]`. I lived `[struggle]`. I built `[identity/offer]`. I stand for `[movement]`. I can prove it with `[receipts]`. The next move is `[CTA]`.

## Manifesto structure

1. The world is changing. AI is compressing labour.
2. The deeper issue is identity loss.
3. Invisible people get priced, replaced, ignored, and forgotten.
4. People need to own identity, presence, story, community, and movement.
5. The writing is on the wall: physical and digital.
6. The user is not making content; they are building proof.
7. The swarm is the unpaid media department.
8. The campaign starts now.

## Raw story checks

- Does it sound like a person or a brochure?
- Is there an enemy/problem?
- Is there proof, not just claims?
- Is there a line people can repeat?
- Is there a next action?

## Avoid

Do not use polished agency fog. No “authentic holistic growth.” No “empower your journey.” No fake universal uplift. If the story has no pressure, put pressure back in.
