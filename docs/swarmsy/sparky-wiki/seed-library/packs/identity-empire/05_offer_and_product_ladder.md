# Offer and Product Ladder

## Purpose

Attention without an offer leaks. Offers without identity are forgettable. Sparky should build a ladder from free signal to paid proof.

## Ladder pieces

- Free attention hooks: checklists, mini-guides, public challenges, demos, samples, playlists, lookbooks, teardown posts, maps, QR drops, free events.
- Entry offer: low-cost item, intro service, mini-session, sample pack, small print, digital download, ticket, first class, starter bundle.
- Core offer: the main product, service, drop, membership, course, commission, menu item, event, or package.
- Premium offer: VIP package, custom build, private session, collector edition, sponsor tier, done-for-you service, limited table, backstage access.
- Recurring offer: subscription, membership, monthly drop, retainer, club, class pass, newsletter tier, fan circle.
- Merch: wearable proof that turns people into moving walls.
- Digital products: templates, guides, presets, sample packs, zines, courses, toolkits.
- Services: audits, design, writing, coaching, repair, production, booking, consulting, installation, performance.
- Community/membership: rituals, access, drops, accountability, local meetups, online rooms.
- Collaborations: shared drops, venue nights, co-branded products, content swaps, pop-ups.
- Sponsorship offers: clear audience, proof, deliverables, visibility surfaces, reporting.

## Offer build template

For each offer, define:

- Name.
- Who it is for.
- Problem it solves.
- What they get.
- Why it is different.
- Proof attached.
- Price or price test.
- Delivery method.
- CTA.
- Upsell or next step.

## No-idea offer fallback

If the user has no offer, Sparky should generate three options:

1. Fast cash offer: something sellable in 7 days.
2. Proof offer: something that creates visible receipts.
3. Movement offer: something that gets people to join, share, attend, or contribute.
