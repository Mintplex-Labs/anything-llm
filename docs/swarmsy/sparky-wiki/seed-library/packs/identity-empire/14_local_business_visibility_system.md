# Local Business Visibility System

## Purpose

For cafés, barbers, gyms, takeaways, tattoo studios, local shops, trades, markets, restaurants, and events. Local businesses do not need bland brand theatre. They need people nearby to notice, trust, visit, return, and talk.

## Local business types

- Cafés.
- Barbers.
- Gyms.
- Takeaways.
- Tattoo studios.
- Local shops.
- Trades.
- Markets.
- Restaurants.
- Events.

## Build system

- Local offer: clear reason to walk in, book, call, order, attend, or bring a friend.
- Local proof: photos, before/after, customer quotes, busy moments, menu/product/service proof, staff story.
- Reviews: ask after the win; make the review route simple.
- Window campaign: permission-owned window message, QR, offer, slogan, proof.
- Flyers: lawful handouts or approved placements with one offer and one QR.
- Social posts: daily proof from the shop floor, not stock-photo lies.
- Local PR: founder story, neighbourhood angle, job/community angle, comeback story, launch/drop/event.
- Collaborations: neighbouring businesses, local artists, gyms, venues, colleges, teams, markets.
- Repeat customer loop: receipt insert, loyalty card, SMS/email/list, “bring one person,” next offer.

## Local campaign examples

- Café: “Your 8AM has a headquarters.” Morning proof posts, window QR, commuter offer, local office flyer with permission.
- Barber: “Fresh cuts. No weak mirrors.” Before/after proof, referral card, local team partnership.
- Gym: “No fake motivation. Show up.” Member proof, challenge board, local business wellness offer.
- Takeaway: “Late-night hunger has a name.” Menu proof, delivery inserts, local student/community posts.
- Tattoo studio: “Wear the story.” Artist flash drop, healed-work proof, booking CTA.
- Trades: “Receipts beat talk.” Before/after proof, local review loop, emergency offer.

## Daily local checklist

- Capture one proof photo/video.
- Post one local story.
- Ask one happy customer for a review.
- Contact one local node.
- Repeat the offer.
- Track visits, calls, DMs, bookings, reviews, and mentions.
