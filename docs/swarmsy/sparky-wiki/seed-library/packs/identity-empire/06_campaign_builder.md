# Campaign Builder

## Purpose

A campaign is repeated pressure around one message. A campaign without repetition is just a post.

## Sparky should create

- Campaign name.
- Campaign enemy/problem.
- Slogan bank.
- Visual direction.
- Content pillars.
- Launch arc.
- Daily actions.
- Call to action.
- Proof capture plan.
- Follow-up plan.
- Repeat cycle.

## Campaign spine

1. Problem: what is being attacked?
2. Name: what should people remember?
3. Slogan: what can they repeat?
4. Proof: what shows it is real?
5. Surfaces: where will it appear?
6. Swarm: who helps spread it?
7. CTA: what should people do?
8. Signal: what proves traction?

## Campaign types

- Artist drop: reveal story, preview work, collector/fan CTA, proof of process, launch day, follow-up.
- Music release: hook line, cover art, snippet run, BTS, local angle, playlist/community outreach, release-day swarm.
- Merch drop: slogan, product proof, model shots, pre-order, street-safe visibility, buyer photos.
- Café/local business launch: window campaign, local offer, menu proof, neighbourhood nodes, review loop.
- Event/exhibition: theme, poster system, artist/founder quote, RSVP, venue proof, recap.
- Personal brand relaunch: new name, enemy, manifesto, content pillars, proof stack, first 30 days.
- Activist/movement visibility: lawful message discipline, community asks, public proof, safety boundaries, partner nodes.
- Online-only campaign: one message across feeds, blogs, forums, newsletters, communities, video, comments, and search.
- Physical + digital campaign: permission-safe physical surfaces paired with QR, hashtag, landing page, posts, and proof capture.

## Shock-safe rule

Shock can be tension, contrast, blunt truth, visual surprise, or cultural friction. It must not become illegal instructions, threats, harassment, vandalism, trespass, evasion, or targeted abuse.
