# Digital Wall Distribution

## Purpose

The internet is an infinite wall. Sparky should turn one message into repeated presence across feeds, search, forums, newsletters, marketplaces, blogs, comments, communities, video platforms, and networks.

## Core platforms

- Instagram: visuals, reels, carousels, stories, collaborations.
- TikTok: fast hooks, proof clips, raw BTS, trends only when current and checked.
- X: sharp lines, launch threads, founder commentary, quote posts.
- Facebook: local groups, event pages, community posts, business pages.
- YouTube: shorts, long proof videos, launch docs, playlists.
- Reddit: value-first posts in relevant communities; follow current community rules.
- Discord: community rooms, launch squad, fan feedback.
- Telegram: broadcast lists, drop alerts, street-team coordination where lawful.
- Substack: essays, launch notes, weekly mission, proof archive.
- Medium: long-form story, founder essays, searchable proof.

## Distribution tools

- SEO: pages and posts that answer what the audience searches.
- Hashtags: scene tags, local tags, campaign tags; avoid spam.
- Comment strategy: useful, sharp comments that add value and carry the name.
- Forum strategy: answer first, promote only when relevant and allowed.
- Community drops: share the right asset in the right room with context.
- Marketplace listings: products/services need story, proof, photos, and search terms.
- Blog outreach: pitch proof and local/cultural angle.
- Newsletter: own the list; platforms can bury you.

## One message, 20 surfaces

Take one campaign line and place it as:

1. Instagram reel.
2. Instagram carousel.
3. Instagram story.
4. TikTok clip.
5. YouTube short.
6. YouTube community post.
7. X post.
8. X thread.
9. Facebook page post.
10. Facebook local group post where allowed.
11. Reddit value post where rules allow.
12. Discord announcement.
13. Telegram broadcast.
14. Substack note.
15. Newsletter section.
16. Blog post.
17. Marketplace listing update.
18. Comment reply.
19. Partner post.
20. Landing page/SEO block.

## Daily distribution checklist

- Post one proof item.
- Repeat one slogan.
- Make one offer CTA.
- Reply to comments.
- Drop value in one community.
- DM one collaborator or node.
- Capture signal: views, saves, replies, clicks, DMs, sales, signups.

Use API/web only for current platform rules, live trends, current competitor checks, and live market research when enabled.
