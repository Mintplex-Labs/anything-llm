# Sparky Prompt Recipes

## Purpose

Prompt recipes users can paste into Sparky when they want the Identity Empire brain. Sparky should answer locally first with Ollama and this wiki pack unless live/current research is explicitly needed and API/web is enabled.

## Core prompts

- “Build my identity empire from nothing.”
- “Turn my messy idea into a brand.”
- “Create a 30-day launch plan.”
- “Make me a shock campaign that stays lawful.”
- “Write 20 slogans.”
- “Build my PR story.”
- “Create my local visibility plan.”
- “Create my digital wall plan.”
- “Act as GHOST and write my press angle.”
- “Act as STICKUP and build my campaign visuals.”
- “Act as SIGNAL and tell me what to measure.”

## Expanded prompts

### Build my identity empire from nothing

> I have no idea what to build. Interview me, extract signal from weak answers, then create my identity, story, offer ladder, campaign name, slogan bank, content calendar, lawful physical visibility plan, digital wall plan, swarm brief, and next 7 actions. Keep it raw and direct.

### Turn my messy idea into a brand

> Here is my messy idea: `[paste idea]`. Build brand name options, alias/persona, tagline, mission, enemy/problem, audience, promise, tone, visual world, offer, proof stack, one-line pitch, 30-second pitch, and long-form brand description.

### Make me a shock campaign that stays lawful

> Build a blunt campaign around `[idea]` that creates attention without illegal instructions, vandalism, trespass, evasion, harassment, or fake claims. Include campaign name, slogans, visual direction, lawful physical visibility, digital distribution, proof capture, and CTA.

### Act as GHOST

> Act as GHOST mode. Turn this launch into a public narrative. Write a press release, founder quote, journalist pitch, local press angle, podcast pitch, collaboration DM, sponsor pitch, launch announcement, media kit checklist, and controversy-safe shock angle.

### Act as STICKUP

> Act as STICKUP mode. Build campaign visuals and slogans for `[brand/campaign]`. Give poster concepts, feed concepts, lawful physical visibility ideas, launch moves, repetition rules, and proof capture.

### Act as SWARMNET

> Act as SWARMNET mode. Build my distribution map across platforms, communities, partners, local nodes, launch squad, ambassadors, referrals, and one-message-20-surfaces plan.

### Act as SIGNAL

> Act as SIGNAL mode. Here are my numbers and reactions: `[paste metrics]`. Tell me what to repeat, kill, and test next. Give me the next 24-hour action.
