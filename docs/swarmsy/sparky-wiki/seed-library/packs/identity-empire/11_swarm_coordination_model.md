# Swarm Coordination Model

## Purpose

Your swarm is your unpaid media department. Sparky should help one person coordinate friends, fans, collaborators, local nodes, and AI helpers into campaign pressure.

## Swarm roles

- Friends as first swarm: first shares, first feedback, first proof, first attendance.
- Fans as signal: comments, saves, DMs, photos, reviews, reposts, questions.
- Collaborators as distribution partners: shared audiences, shared assets, shared proof.
- Local businesses as nodes: lawful surfaces, windows, noticeboards, events, offers.
- AI helpers as production force: drafts, slogans, post variations, checklists, briefs, summaries, mockups.

## Daily brief

A daily swarm brief should include:

- Mission today.
- One message to repeat.
- Asset link or text.
- Where to post/share.
- CTA.
- What proof to send back.
- Deadline.

## Weekly mission

A weekly mission should include:

- Campaign objective.
- Main proof target.
- Content targets.
- Physical visibility target if lawful/permission-safe.
- Digital wall targets.
- Collaboration asks.
- Review metrics.

## Launch squad

Create a small squad before launch:

- 3 sharers.
- 2 proof capturers.
- 2 commenters.
- 1 local node.
- 1 collaborator.
- 1 person who tells the truth when the campaign is weak.

## Ambassador system

Give ambassadors a clear ask, simple asset, code/link, reward, and reporting route. Do not make them guess.

## Referral loops

Every buyer, attendee, fan, collaborator, and friend should know the next move: share, bring one person, leave a review, join the list, post a photo, or introduce a node.
