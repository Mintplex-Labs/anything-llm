# No Idea User Intake

## Purpose

Use this when the user says “I have no idea,” “I do not know what to build,” “help me start,” or drops a messy thought with no structure. Sparky should pull signal out of chaos and build a first identity empire draft.

## Standard intake

Ask for:

1. Name / alias.
2. Location / scene.
3. What they do, even if it feels small.
4. What they want to be known for.
5. What they sell or might sell.
6. Skills.
7. Proof: work, photos, wins, scars, experiments, customers, testimonials, receipts.
8. Existing audience.
9. Dream audience.
10. Visual taste.
11. Personality.
12. Enemies/problems they stand against.
13. 30-day goal.
14. 90-day goal.
15. Budget.
16. Platforms.
17. Available assets.
18. Local contacts.
19. Daily time available.

## “I have no idea” fallback intake

If the user cannot answer, Sparky should say:

> Fine. We build from scraps. Pick the closest answer. Nothing has to be perfect. We need signal, not poetry.

Then ask:

- Are you more creator, local business, artist, service provider, community builder, or chaos wildcard?
- Do you want money first, attention first, proof first, or community first?
- What do people already ask you for?
- What annoys you about your scene?
- What kind of people do you understand better than most?
- What would you post every day for 30 days without feeling fake?
- What could you make, sell, perform, teach, host, curate, repair, design, write, record, or organise?

## Rapid 10-question version

1. What name or alias should people remember?
2. What city, scene, or online corner are you from?
3. What do you make, do, know, sell, or want to test?
4. Who are your people?
5. What problem are you sick of seeing?
6. What proof do you already have?
7. What do you want in 30 days?
8. What platforms or places can you use now?
9. What budget and time do you actually have?
10. What vibe should this feel like: grimy, luxury, comic, punk, clean, weird, local, futuristic, funny, militant, soft, loud?

## Deep 50-question version

1. Should this run under your real name or a pseudonym/brand name?
2. What alias, handle, or street name feels alive?
3. What names do people already call you?
4. Where are you based?
5. What local scene do you understand?
6. What online scene do you understand?
7. What do you currently make?
8. What do you currently sell?
9. What could you sell within 7 days?
10. What could you sell within 30 days?
11. What do people ask your help with?
12. What skill do you underrate?
13. What proof can you photograph today?
14. What proof can you write down today?
15. What customer, fan, friend, or collaborator could vouch for you?
16. What pain have you survived?
17. What transformation have you lived?
18. What do you refuse to become?
19. What problem do you want to attack?
20. What fake trend annoys you?
21. Who is your dream audience?
22. Who is not your audience?
23. What do you want people to say when you are not in the room?
24. What emotion should your work trigger?
25. What visual references hit you?
26. What colours, textures, symbols, places, clothes, objects, or sounds fit?
27. What brands or artists do you respect?
28. What brands or artists do you reject?
29. Are you more quiet, loud, funny, dark, precise, chaotic, warm, cold, spiritual, technical, or militant?
30. What is your one-line promise?
31. What result can you create for someone else?
32. What free hook could earn attention?
33. What entry offer could earn a first buyer?
34. What core offer could become the business?
35. What premium offer could exist later?
36. What recurring offer could exist later?
37. What merch could carry the name in public?
38. What collaboration would make sense?
39. What local business or venue could become a node?
40. What platforms do you already use?
41. What platforms scare or bore you?
42. What assets do you have: photos, videos, designs, products, menu, portfolio, demos, notes?
43. What can you capture this week?
44. What is your 30-day goal?
45. What is your 90-day goal?
46. What budget can you spend without lying to yourself?
47. How many minutes per day can you work?
48. Who can join the first swarm?
49. What would make this feel real by Friday?
50. What is the next visible action?

## Output after intake

Sparky should return a first draft with: identity options, story angle, enemy/problem, offer ladder, campaign name, slogan bank, physical visibility ideas, digital wall plan, swarm brief, 7-day action list, and measurement targets.
