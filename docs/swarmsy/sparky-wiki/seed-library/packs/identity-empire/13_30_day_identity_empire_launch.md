# 30-Day Identity Empire Launch

## Purpose

A full 30-day plan for a user starting from nothing or relaunching from scraps. Week 1 builds identity and story. Week 2 builds offer and assets. Week 3 builds campaign and distribution. Week 4 launches, captures proof, follows up, and repeats.

## Week 1: identity and story

Day 1: Complete rapid intake. Pick working name, audience, enemy/problem, and 30-day goal.
Day 2: Generate 10 names, 5 taglines, 3 persona options, and choose one temporary identity.
Day 3: Write origin story, founder story, and why-now angle.
Day 4: Build manifesto: what you reject, what you stand for, what people should do.
Day 5: Define visual world: colours, symbols, textures, photo style, references, no-go clichés.
Day 6: Create proof inventory: photos, testimonials, work samples, scars, process, assets.
Day 7: Publish first proof post and one identity statement. Review signal.

## Week 2: offer and assets

Day 8: Build free hook, entry offer, core offer, premium idea, recurring idea.
Day 9: Choose one offer to test in 7 days. Write one-line and 30-second pitch.
Day 10: Create landing page copy or simple post-based offer page.
Day 11: Create first asset set: logo/type treatment placeholder, poster, carousel, short video script.
Day 12: Create media kit basics: bio, photos, proof, quote, links.
Day 13: Build content calendar for the next 14 days.
Day 14: Soft-test offer with 5 people. Capture objections and proof.

## Week 3: campaign and distribution

Day 15: Name the campaign. Define enemy/problem, slogan bank, CTA, proof target.
Day 16: Build “one message, 20 surfaces” distribution plan.
Day 17: Recruit launch squad: friends, fans, collaborator, local node.
Day 18: Build lawful physical visibility plan: windows, noticeboards, event signage, flyers, QR, merch.
Day 19: Write PR angle, launch announcement, collaboration DM, sponsor pitch.
Day 20: Post campaign teaser and proof thread.
Day 21: Review signal. Cut weak lines. Double down on strongest slogan and proof.

## Week 4: launch, proof, follow-up

Day 22: Launch countdown day 1: problem and manifesto.
Day 23: Countdown day 2: founder story and proof.
Day 24: Countdown day 3: offer reveal and CTA.
Day 25: Countdown day 4: collaborator/fan/local proof.
Day 26: Countdown day 5: behind-the-scenes and objection killer.
Day 27: Countdown day 6: final reminder and swarm brief.
Day 28: Launch day: post, email/newsletter, community drops, squad shares, proof capture.
Day 29: Follow up: thank, repost, answer DMs, ask for reviews, collect photos, sell again.
Day 30: SIGNAL review: views, clicks, DMs, signups, sales, attendance, saves, shares, local recognition. Choose repeat, kill, and test-next moves.

## End-of-month output

Sparky should produce a review sheet, strongest content list, updated offer, next campaign name, and 7-day repeat plan.
