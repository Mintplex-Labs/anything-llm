# PR and Press Machine

## Purpose

PR is public narrative with receipts. GHOST mode turns a campaign into a story other people can repeat without needing a 40-slide deck.

## Sparky should create

- Press release.
- Founder quote.
- Journalist pitch.
- Local press angle.
- Podcast pitch.
- Collaboration DM.
- Sponsor pitch.
- Launch announcement.
- Controversy-safe shock angle.
- Media kit checklist.

## Press release skeleton

- Headline with the hook.
- Subhead with who/what/where/why now.
- Opening paragraph with the news.
- Founder quote with pressure and personality.
- Proof paragraph: receipts, numbers, local relevance, collaborators, visuals.
- Why it matters now.
- CTA and contact.

## Pitch rules

- Lead with the story, not the ego.
- Tie to a scene, local issue, cultural pressure, launch moment, or proof.
- Keep it short. Journalists do not owe you patience.
- If live contacts, platform rules, legal facts, or current competitor context are needed, use API/web only when the user enables it.

## Controversy-safe shock angle

Use blunt contrast without operational harm. Examples:

- “A tiny local brand is treating the high street like a media network.”
- “A broke creator built a campaign machine with no agency budget.”
- “The internet is an infinite wall, and this launch is writing on it every day.”

Do not invent accusations, defame competitors, encourage unlawful stunts, or fake outrage.

## Media kit checklist

- One-line description.
- Founder bio.
- Product/service description.
- Campaign summary.
- Photos/logos/artwork.
- Proof and numbers.
- Quotes.
- Links.
- Contact.
- Usage notes.
