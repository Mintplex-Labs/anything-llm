# Brand Foundation Builder

## Purpose

Turn scraps into a brand foundation. Not a corporate mood board. A usable identity system people can repeat, buy from, join, and spread.

## Build outputs

Sparky should create:

- Brand name ideas: 10 rough, 5 sharper, 3 final contenders.
- Alias/persona: the human face or operating mask.
- Tagline: short line with bite.
- Mission: what the name is here to do.
- Enemy/problem: the force the brand fights.
- Audience: the people who feel seen by it.
- Promise: what changes when people engage.
- Tone of voice: words, rhythm, taboos, signature lines.
- Visual world: colours, textures, symbols, camera style, street references, digital references.
- Offer: what people can buy, join, book, attend, collect, or share.
- Proof stack: receipts, work, photos, testimonials, experiments, failures, wins.
- One-line pitch.
- 30-second pitch.
- Long-form brand description.

## Foundation formula

Name + enemy + promise + proof + repeated visual language = memory.

## Naming rules

A strong name should be easy to say, easy to tag, hard to confuse, and capable of carrying slogans. Sparky should check for obvious risk only from available local knowledge unless API/web is enabled for live availability checks.

## Persona rules

The persona should sharpen the real user, not fake a cartoon. Use the user’s scars, taste, location, obsession, humour, and refusal.

## Pitch templates

One-line pitch:

> `[Name]` helps `[audience]` fight `[enemy/problem]` by `[offer/system]`, backed by `[proof]`.

30-second pitch:

> I built `[Name]` because `[problem]` keeps making people invisible. We serve `[audience]` with `[offer]`. The difference is `[edge]`. You can see the proof in `[proof stack]`. The next move is `[CTA]`.

Long-form description should include origin, enemy, audience, offer, proof, movement, and next action.
