# Archive Editing Rules

These rules are for SWARMSY community archive editing.

- This is not strict Wikipedia formatting.
- Not every paragraph needs a citation.
- Important factual claims need source cards.
- Names, dates, prices, events, legal claims, public statements, and direct quotes need source cards.
- Use provenance labels (cited evidence, founder thesis, myth/lore, disputed, needs-source).
- No unsupported factual certainty.
- Respect safety boundaries; no operational illegal guidance.
