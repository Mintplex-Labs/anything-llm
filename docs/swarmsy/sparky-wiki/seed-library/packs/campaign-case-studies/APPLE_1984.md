# Apple “1984” — Campaign Case Study

## One-line summary

Apple positioned Macintosh as liberation from conformity, using a cinematic Super Bowl ad that turned a computer launch into a cultural event.

## Why it worked

- clear enemy: conformity / Big Brother / category dominance
- cinematic execution
- launch timing around Super Bowl attention
- scarcity of airing increased myth
- product became part of a bigger cultural story

## SPARKY Wiki translation

For a user brand:

```txt
old world enemy → user belief → bold symbol → launch moment → proof/action
```

Example:

- Enemy: boring local ads
- Belief: your shop has a story
- Symbol: one bold window poster
- Launch: Friday reveal with QR campaign
- Action: join the local offer / follow / book

## Boundary

Do not create false enemies or manipulate fear. Make the enemy a problem, not a protected person/group.
