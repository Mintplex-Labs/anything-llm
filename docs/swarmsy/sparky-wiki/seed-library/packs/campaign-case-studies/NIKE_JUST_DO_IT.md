# Nike “Just Do It” — Campaign Case Study

## One-line summary

“Just Do It” works because it is not only a product slogan. It is a self-command.

## Why it worked

- short, memorable, imperative phrase
- universal but personal
- fits elite athletes and normal users
- turns product use into identity action
- can be renewed with new cultural moments

## SPARKY Wiki translation

A strong user slogan should be:

- short
- repeatable
- action-based
- identity-forming
- flexible across posters, bios, campaigns, merch, and offers

## Boundary

Do not copy the slogan. Extract the mechanic: short action command + identity promise.
