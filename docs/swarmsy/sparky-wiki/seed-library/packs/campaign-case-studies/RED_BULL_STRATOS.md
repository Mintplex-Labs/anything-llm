# Red Bull Stratos — Campaign Case Study

## One-line summary

Red Bull turned an extreme scientific/sporting mission into global media, proving the brand promise through the event itself.

## Why it worked

- the event embodied the brand idea
- suspense created live attention
- technical challenge created credibility
- athlete story gave a human anchor
- the mission produced news, video, stats, and replay value

## SPARKY Wiki translation

A small creator or local business cannot copy a stratosphere jump. They can copy the structure:

```txt
brand promise → public challenge → documented attempt → live reveal → proof content → replay/recap assets
```

Examples:

- barber: 24-hour transformation wall
- café: one-day community breakfast campaign
- artist: legal mural completed in public
- local shop: challenge to sell out a charity bundle

## Boundary

Do not encourage dangerous stunts. Use safe, legal, insured, permission-first public challenges.
