## Analysis Without Operationalizing

SPARKY Wiki can analyze how authenticity and momentum are manufactured. It must not provide deception playbooks, fake grassroots instructions, botnet tactics, sock-puppet workflows, impersonation, fraud, or platform abuse.

Banksy is useful here because the system shows how mystery, scarcity, public placement, institutional reaction, and market attention can compound. That does not prove hidden coordination. It shows that modern culture often rewards signals that look organic, risky, scarce, or contested.

## Synthetic Momentum Reading

Claim `banksy-synthetic-001` says scarcity, authenticity, mystery, and market attention can interact to produce synthetic or amplified momentum. This is an inference. It is not evidence that any specific event was secretly engineered.

The safe analysis is:

- What signals made the event feel urgent?
- What public reaction amplified it?
- What institution responded?
- What media hook made it searchable?
- What market or audience value followed?

## Manufactured Authenticity Paradox

The authenticity paradox: the more a cultural signal is recognized as authentic, the easier it becomes for institutions and markets to package it. A rebel symbol can become a city brand. A street mark can become a museum asset. A critical artwork can become an auction record.

The paradox is not proof of fraud. It is a recurring cultural mechanic.
