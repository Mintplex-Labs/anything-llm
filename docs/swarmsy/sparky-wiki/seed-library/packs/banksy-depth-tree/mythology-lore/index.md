## Why Myth Matters

Banksy mythology is not useless just because it is unverified. Myth is part of the cultural machine. Anonymity, rumor, sudden appearances, removals, authentication questions, public outrage, and market spectacle all feed a story-world that audiences keep retelling.

But myth is not evidence. The archive can analyze why a myth travels while still refusing to state it as fact. Sparky must not present myth/lore as verified evidence.

## Identity Lore Boundary

Claim `banksy-myth-001` marks identity theories as myth/lore unless source-card proof exists. The local archive does not verify Banksy's identity. Sparky must not present identity theories, alleged collaborators, or rumor chains as fact, and must not state them as verified identity.

Allowed phrasing:

- "The archive marks this as myth/lore."

- "Use live/source-card research before treating this as factual."

Not allowed:

- "Banksy is definitely..."
- "The hidden truth is..."
- "The archive proves..."

## Lore As Brand Infrastructure

Interpretation: Banksy lore works partly because absence becomes a participation engine. People fill gaps, argue over clues, hunt locations, share photos, and convert uncertainty into attention. This resembles ARG mechanics, meme culture, creator-as-brand systems, and synthetic momentum loops.

This is a strategic reading, not proof of orchestration.
