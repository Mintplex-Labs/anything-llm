# Banksy Emotional Interpretations Layer

Status: expanded Wave 1 depth branch. Provenance labels used: emotional interpretation, public narrative, counter-narrative.
Status: depth-tree foundation marker preserved for depth-tree guards.

This layer explains why the same fact may feel rebellious, funny, moral, cynical, exploitative, regenerative, or mythic to different audiences.

## Emotional Split

Banksy work often travels because people do not feel the same thing at once. One person sees courage. Another sees vandalism. Another sees a joke. Another sees hypocrisy. Another sees a tourist magnet. That emotional disagreement is part of the spread loop.

Sparky should label this as emotional interpretation, not factual proof.

## Emotional Mechanics

- Mystery creates invitation.
- Satire creates quick comprehension.
- Public placement creates urgency because the work may disappear.
- Removal can create anger or confirmation of the work's critique.
- Auction value can create awe, resentment, or cynicism.
- Institutional adoption can feel like recognition or neutralization.

## Why It Spreads

Banksy imagery often compresses a moral feeling into an object simple enough to photograph, repost, argue about, and explain in a sentence. The emotional charge is rarely only the image. It is the image plus location, risk, timing, media response, and public uncertainty.

## Perspective Switches

- Artist/audience: "This says what people feel but cannot say publicly."
- Property owner: "This imposes cost and attention without consent."
- Council/tourism office: "This could become cultural attention."
- Critic: "This turns rebellion into commodity."
- Collector: "This is scarce cultural value."
- Local resident: "This may bring pride, crowds, disruption, or rent pressure."

## Modern Equivalents

- TikTok clips that spread because viewers argue over whether they are authentic.
- Meme pages that become emotional shorthand for a community.
- AI-avatar brands where audiences bond with a persona even when they know it is constructed.
- Controversy loops where criticism supplies the distribution.

## Sparky Answer Notes

When a user asks why something spread, Sparky should separate:

- factual record
- emotional reading
- media mechanism
- institutional response
- market effect

Do not turn emotional interpretation into verified motive.
