# Banksy Institutional Response Layer

Status: depth-tree foundation marker preserved for depth-tree guards.

This layer compares council, market, media, collector, property-owner, police/security, tourism, and public responses across contexts.

## The Institution Does Not Have One Response

Banksy exposes institutional inconsistency because different institutions respond to different forms of value. A police/security response may see criminal damage. A council tourism department may see footfall. A museum may see audience development. A collector may see scarcity. A property owner may see cost, liability, or opportunity. A newspaper may see conflict.

This is not hypocrisy in every case; sometimes it is different institutional roles colliding. Sparky should avoid lazy certainty and explain the incentives.

## Removal, Preservation, Exhibition

`banksy-source-conflict-001` gives the _Slave Labour_ controversy as a source-conflict anchor: a public-wall work was reported as removed/disappeared and connected to auction controversy. `banksy-institution-002` gives _Season's Greetings_ as a public wall to museum/protection case. `banksy-institution-003` shows _Cut & Run_ as museum-scale institutional display with recorded visitor attention.

These cases show three response modes:

1. Removal/extraction: the object leaves the wall and becomes a saleable or protected thing.
2. Preservation/display: the object is stabilized as cultural material.
3. Institutional programming: the artist's archive becomes a managed visitor experience.

## Political Space

`banksy-institution-001` anchors the Walled Off Hotel as a public reported Banksy project in Bethlehem beside the separation wall. This is institutional response at a geopolitical edge: the work is not only art tourism, not only hotel, not only protest, and not only spectacle. It sits inside political geography, tourism economy, media representation, and critique.

Sparky must avoid flattening this into "Banksy did a hotel stunt." It should ask: who is represented, who benefits, who is made visible, who is commodified, and who may disagree?

## Institutional Response Shift

Over time, the response pattern can shift:

- first: remove or punish unsanctioned mark
- then: protect or monetize recognized work
- then: commission similar aesthetics
- then: package the area as creative
- then: build tourism, museum, or developer narrative around the style

This timeline is an interpretation. Use local case citations before presenting it as specific institutional fact.
