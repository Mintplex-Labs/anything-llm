# Banksy

## Wiki Formula Status

Subject id: `graffiti.banksy`

This root page is the readable overview and branch doorway for the local Banksy wiki. It is not meant to hold every detail. Deep factual, market, legal, symbol, council, regeneration, and disputed-claim analysis lives in the branch pages listed below.

## Confidence Model

- verified fact: source-backed public facts such as dates, artworks, exhibitions, auctions, named places, public statements, and documented removals.
- cited claim: important claim with source-card IDs in `claim-map.md`.
- disputed claim: repeated claim or theory marked disputed and never stated as certainty.
- inference: SPARKY Wiki pattern analysis from observable evidence.
- interpretation: cultural, symbolic, economic, or campaign analysis.
- speculation: possible reading that needs source work or live lookup.
- cultural myth / lore: immersive subject lore around the public image, clearly marked as myth/lore rather than fact.

## What Is Verified Vs Disputed

Verified/cited baseline: Banksy is treated here as a pseudonymous street artist with public works, authenticated works, market events, exhibitions/projects, and public/media traces that require source cards for high-impact factual claims.

Disputed: identity theories, direct influence claims, hidden collaboration claims, and claims about private intent remain disputed or needs-source unless tied to strong public evidence.
