# Banksy Narrative Analysis Layer

Status: expanded Wave 1 depth branch. Provenance labels used: public narrative, counter-narrative, interpretation, myth/lore.
Status: depth-tree foundation marker preserved for depth-tree guards.

This layer tracks hero/villain framing, public narrative, counter-narrative, ambiguity, status storytelling, and archive memory.

## Narrative Positions

Banksy can be narrated as:

- rebel artist
- vandal
- moral satirist
- market phenomenon
- civic tourism asset
- anonymous brand system
- institutional contradiction machine
- myth/lore generator

None of these is the whole truth. Each is a perspective that highlights one part of the system.

## Hero Narrative

The hero narrative says Banksy punctures hypocrisy, exposes power, and gives public language to shared discomfort. This reading often powers emotional spread. It is strongest when tied to cited public works or events, not free-floating admiration.

## Counter-Narrative

The counter-narrative says Banksy benefits from the same attention economy and market systems being criticized. Auction records, museum-scale exhibitions, and tourism effects make this criticism harder to dismiss.

## Public Narrative Versus Archive Truth

SPARKY Wiki should allow public narrative while preserving archive labels:

- public narrative: what people repeat
- cited claim: what a source card supports
- interpretation: what the archive infers
- myth/lore: what circulates without verification
- counter-narrative: what challenges the dominant story

## Narrative Evolution

The narrative can shift over time:

1. unsanctioned mark
2. local curiosity
3. media event
4. tourist destination
5. market object
6. museum/council/civic asset
7. disputed memory

This timeline is a reusable interpretive model. Use case-specific citations before presenting it as factual sequence for a particular work.

## Sparky Answer Notes

Sparky should answer narrative prompts by switching lenses:

- audience story
- property story
- council story
- market story
- critic story
- myth/lore story

Then it should say which lenses are cited and which are interpretation.
