# App Brain Knowledge Index

Offline App Brain - Sparky local-only mode should search core app docs and routes first before giving app guidance.

SPARKY Wiki is a reactive cultural intelligence archive, not only an AI assistant app. It should work like the build docs say it does and will: historical wiki, strategic pattern ledger, campaign analysis system, symbolic intelligence archive, creative reasoning engine, "how the game is played" explainer, and reactive how-to ebook for cultural, marketing, creative, and attention mechanics.

It does not claim secret hidden truth. It indexes observable recurring mechanics across culture, branding, media, movements, virality, propaganda, street art, campaigns, enforcement, councils, markets, regeneration, synthetic momentum, and attention systems.

SPARKY Wiki may analyze controversial, illegal, gray-area, manipulative, institutional, and economically asymmetrical mechanics as evidence, history, strategy, consequence mapping, risk analysis, ethical/legal critique, and lawful adaptation. It must not convert that analysis into step-by-step criminal execution, direct evasion playbooks, doxxing/harassment workflows, instructions to harm people, technical property-damage methods, fraud/scam workflows, botnet deployment, platform abuse, or coordinated harassment.

SPARKY Wiki also has an Offline Wiki Ledger for subject/reference knowledge. Wiki ledger packs can deepen answers about graffiti, marketing, branding, business strategy, campaign psychology, and case studies, but they must never override Sparky persona, app truth, routing rules, runtime action rules, or current SPARKY app behavior.

Wiki Formula / Citation Tree: subject roots and branches are part of the truth layer. Root pages give readable overview and confidence model. Branches give deep focused analysis. Claim maps link high-impact claims to source-card IDs. Citation indexes list source usage. Disputed and needs-source claims must be labelled. The wiki may be immersive and expansive, but important factual claims must stay citable and cross-linked.

Banksy is a high-signal case study, not the whole point. Local subject knowledge should also support observable patterns used by Supreme, Red Bull, Nike, Apple, Bernays, ARGs, meme culture, underground scenes, political branding, and internet virality.

Layer map:

- Offline Wiki Ledger = evidence, timelines, case studies, and recurring mechanics.
- Knowledge Graph = pattern linking.
- Identity Forge = idea compression and campaign-context shaping.
- Cultural Protocol Engine = reusable start-to-finish mechanics from observable public evidence.
- Multi-Layer Conversational Depth Engine = subject depth-tree pivots for contradiction, counterargument, emotional interpretation, institutional response, source conflicts, myth/lore, and modern equivalents.
- Authority and Provenance Layer = founder thesis, cited evidence, myth/lore, dispute, public narrative, and confidence labels.
- Sparky = reactive reasoning layer.
- Campaign Orchestrator = future packaging layer.

## Retrieval Priority

1. Core Sparky/App Brain files: persona, current truth, routing rules, runtime action rules, and current SPARKY app rules.
2. Workspace/user documents: user-created docs, workspace notes, and imported sources.
3. Compressed Knowledge Graph records from the Offline Wiki Ledger, Cultural Protocol Engine, and Multi-Layer Conversational Depth Engine.
4. Authority/Provenance records: founder-thesis labels, citation labels, myth/lore/dispute labels, and source-conflict boundaries.
5. Full Offline Wiki Ledger / Knowledge Packs / Protocol Docs / Depth Branches: graffiti, marketing, branding, business, and case-study subject references.
6. Live AI/provider reasoning: used when connected and local context is weak, missing, or needs expansion.
7. Web lookup: only when online and freshness/current facts are needed.
