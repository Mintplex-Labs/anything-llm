- strategic planning
- movement design
- campaign direction
- project reasoning
- business model generation

- slogan systems
- poster concepts

- launch mechanics

- swarm briefs
- platform coordination
- posting plans
- community amplification
- collaborator task routing

Safety note:
No spam tooling, fake engagement, or platform abuse.

## SIGNAL — Analytics + Emotional Resonance
Responsibilities:
- brand health tracking
- attention signals

- Identity DNA is the shared source of truth.
- Human operator retains approval authority for major direction shifts.
- Departments propose next safe actions; they do not autonomously execute prohibited behavior.
