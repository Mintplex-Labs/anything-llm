# Identity Forge Campaign Intelligence

Status: PR152 foundation. Identity Forge is a local campaign intelligence layer under Sparky. It does not execute campaigns, call providers, call web lookup, build Open Runtime, build a source editor, or touch rewards/backend/Telegram sync/website systems.

## Definition

Identity Forge is not only a form or saved brand profile. It is the offline helper layer that turns fuzzy user ideas into structured campaign context Sparky can reason from.

Street Swarm is a reactive cultural intelligence archive, not just an AI assistant app. Identity Forge is the idea-compression layer inside that archive: it turns the user's brand, product, community, or campaign intent into structured context that can be compared against observable cultural and marketing mechanics.

It does not claim secret hidden truth. It uses the Offline Wiki Ledger and Knowledge Graph as local reference material for evidence, timelines, case studies, recurring mechanics, symbolic patterns, campaign analysis, and "how the game is played" explanations.

Identity Forge sits between:

1. user idea
2. local app wiki and Offline Wiki Ledger
3. Knowledge Graph compression
4. Sparky campaign reasoning
5. future Campaign Orchestrator package output

Core doctrine:

- Offline Wiki Ledger = evidence, timelines, case studies, and recurring mechanics.
- Knowledge Graph = pattern linking.
- Identity Forge = idea compression and campaign-context shaping.
- Cultural Protocol Engine = lawful start-to-finish mechanics for campaign signals, timing, press/search hooks, spread loops, and post-campaign analysis.
- Multi-Layer Conversational Depth Engine = campaign-relevant subject layers for emotional positioning, criticism, regeneration, authenticity, institutional response, audience psychology, and source conflicts.
- Authority and Provenance Layer = founder thesis, cited evidence, myth/lore, dispute, counter-narrative, and source-card labels.
- Sparky = reactive reasoning layer.
- Campaign Orchestrator = future packaging layer.

## Strategy Use

Identity Forge can recommend local packs such as Banksy, Supreme Drop Culture, Nike Just Do It, David Ogilvy, Red Bull Stratos, Apple 1984, and Edward Bernays when the user idea matches their lawful strategy lessons.

Identity Forge can also recommend local cultural protocols:

- Banksy Style Public Signal: lawful public visibility, symbols, timing, earned media, and mystery without doxxing or illegal placement.
- Supreme Drop Scarcity: honest scarcity, release ritual, collaboration signal, and community proof.
- Red Bull Spectacle: safe proof events, countdowns, press hooks, and replay assets.
- Nike Identity Compression: audience tension compressed into a portable line and repeated proof behavior.
- Apple 1984 Category Disruption: category enemy, liberation story, and launch mythology.
- Bernays Public Opinion: public opinion mechanics with disclosure, real authority, and anti-manipulation boundaries.
- Meme Culture Viral Loop: remixable template, emotional compression, and platform-native participation.
- ARG Mystery Trail: fair clue ladder, community solving, archive breadcrumbs, and safe reveals.
- Controlled Rebellion Regeneration: how street culture moves from criminalized expression to sanctioned public art, town/city branding, creative districts, tourism, regeneration, rent pressure, and commercial absorption.
- Synthetic Momentum And Manufactured Authenticity: how aliases, identity layering, lore, paid/seeded amplification, scarcity, and grassroots-looking proof can produce perceived momentum while creating trust/legal risk.

Identity Forge may identify authenticity signaling, outsider positioning, synthetic scarcity, momentum engineering, lore amplification, controversy amplification, coordinated narrative framing, and "appears organic" mechanics. It must frame them analytically and ethically, not as direct operational instructions.

Identity Forge may use the Authority and Provenance Layer to separate founder/operator thesis from cited public evidence, protocol inference, myth/lore, public narrative, and counter-narrative. This matters when campaign context depends on identity, reputation, community memory, authenticity, or disputed claims.

Identity Forge may also recommend depth layers. For example, a public-art regeneration idea can use the regeneration-impact layer, institutional-response layer, economics layer, criticism layer, and source-conflicts layer before Sparky packages any campaign context. This keeps strategy from collapsing into a single inspirational summary.

When Identity Forge recommends a protocol tied to a subject, it may point Sparky toward relevant wiki branches. For Banksy/street-art regeneration ideas, use `docs/wiki/subjects/banksy/councils-regeneration.md`, `gentrification-economics.md`, and `manufactured-authenticity.md` for what happened / who paid / who benefited, then use protocol docs for how the mechanic worked.

Banksy is a high-signal case study, not the whole point. The wider archive should also support observable mechanics from Supreme, Red Bull, Nike, Apple, Bernays, ARGs, meme culture, underground scenes, political branding, and internet virality.

Examples:

- Banksy: symbolic visibility, earned media, public-interest analysis, lawful permission-first adaptation.
- Supreme: honest scarcity, drop rhythm, cultural fit, community ritual.
- Nike: identity compression and repeated proof behavior.
- Ogilvy: research, proof, offer clarity, audience respect.
- Red Bull Stratos: safe spectacle, owned event media, proof and replay.

## Safety Boundary

For graffiti, street art, guerrilla, or stunt campaigns:

- Allowed: legal street art, permissioned murals, stickers/posters where permitted, pop-ups, social/PR concepts, ethical stunts, media/cultural strategy analysis.
- Not allowed: vandalism instructions, trespass, evading police, illegal placement, property damage, doxxing, counterfeit claims, fake authenticity, unsafe stunts, or manipulation that hides sponsorship/authority.

Banksy-style and street-culture knowledge includes real gray-area mechanics such as illegal placement, enforcement asymmetry, cleanup economics, council tolerance, auction dynamics, and controlled rebellion. Identity Forge may analyze those mechanics and map lawful/permissioned equivalents. It must not provide step-by-step illegal execution, direct evasion playbooks, property-damage methods, doxxing/harassment workflows, fraud/scam workflows, botnet deployment, platform abuse, or coordinated harassment.
