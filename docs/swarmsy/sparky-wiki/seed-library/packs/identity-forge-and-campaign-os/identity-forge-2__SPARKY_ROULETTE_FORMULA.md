# Sparky Roulette Formula-Driven Uniqueness

Status: **PLANNED GENERATION ARCHITECTURE**

## Positioning
Sparky Roulette is a CREATE ME path for users who are stuck.

It is **formula-driven uniqueness**, not random idea spinning.

## Input Factors
- personality archetypes
- cultural graph signals
- emotional resonance patterns
- aesthetic combinations
- market gap signals
- movement mechanics
- audience psychology cues
- monetization logic
- symbolic identity systems
- campaign formula templates

## Generation Formula (Conceptual)
1. Build candidate identity vectors from archetype + market + movement intersections.
2. Score candidates for coherence, distinctiveness, and operator fit.
3. Enforce safety and legality filters before output.
4. Produce one primary concept plus variant branches for reroll/mutate/blend.

Pseudo-logic:

`IdentityCandidate = f(Archetype × Culture × Emotion × Aesthetic × MarketGap × Movement × Monetization)`

`FinalOutput = rank(filter(IdentityCandidate, safety + anti-generic constraints), fitScore)`

## Uniqueness Constraints
- avoid generic “content creator” defaults without specific lane definition
- require explicit mission + audience + monetization path coupling
- require at least one differentiating symbolic/aesthetic anchor
- require campaign direction and why-this-fits rationale
- prohibit recycled template phrasing as final output

## Reroll / Mutate / Blend Rules
- **Reroll:** keep operator constraints, regenerate candidate vectors with different weighted intersections.
- **Mutate:** keep core mission but alter one or two dimensions (voice, aesthetic, lane, monetization).
- **Blend:** combine accepted traits from two saved candidates into a reconciled hybrid with conflict notes.

## Output Structure
- identity name
- project concept
- industry lane
- audience
- movement mission
- visual aesthetic
- brand voice
- content strategy
- campaign direction
- slogan stack
- product/service ideas
- monetization paths
- launch plan
- swarm team recommendations
- why this fits
- action options (accept / reroll / mutate / blend / save)

## Guardrails Against Generic Ideas
- enforce lane specificity (who, what, why now)
- enforce narrative stance and tension
- enforce explicit audience and distribution logic
- enforce operator-owned next safe actions

## Guardrails Against Unsafe or Illegal Instructions
Sparky Roulette must refuse or sanitize outputs that include:
- vandalism, sabotage, or evasion tactics
- platform abuse, spam automation, or fake engagement
- harassment, doxxing, or fraud/scam behaviors
- illegal operational guidance of any kind

All physical campaign suggestions must be lawful, permitted, temporary, and brand-safe.
