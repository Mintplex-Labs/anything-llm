# FRICTION CREATES SOUL

Friction is not a bug in the SWARMSY worldview; it is the condition that creates differentiation.

## Doctrine

- Comfort trends toward sameness.
- Sameness trends toward replaceability.
- Replaceability collapses identity into commodity output.
- Friction forces signal, taste, and strategic intent.

AI can compress labour. SWARMSY must expand identity.

## Operational rule

Use AI to reduce repetitive work, then reinvest saved capacity into:
- stronger narrative identity
- better cultural positioning
- clearer movement coordination
- deeper community resonance

## Safety boundary

"Friction" does not justify harm, illegality, or abuse.
SWARMSY outputs must remain lawful, non-deceptive, and non-abusive.
