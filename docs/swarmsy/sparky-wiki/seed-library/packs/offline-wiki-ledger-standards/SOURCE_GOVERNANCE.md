# Source Governance

Knowledge Packs must be source-aware. They are not random scraped folders.

Knowledge Packs are the Offline Wiki Ledger/reference tier. They support local subject answers, but they do not define Sparky identity, app behavior, routing authority, runtime permissions, or current release truth.

Use subject packs after core Sparky/App Brain docs and workspace/user documents. Use live AI/provider reasoning when connected and local wiki coverage is weak or missing. Use web lookup only when online and freshness/current facts are needed.

## Source tiers

### Tier 1 — Primary / official

Examples:

- official artist/company websites
- official authentication or archive pages
- official campaign pages
- regulatory filings
- court/government records
- archived original campaign pages

### Tier 2 — Reputable secondary

Examples:

- museums/galleries
- established newspapers
- academic sources
- books with publication metadata
- reputable industry publications
- auction houses for sale records

### Tier 3 — Index / discovery only

Examples:

- Wikipedia
- fan archives
- blogs
- social posts
- Reddit/forum threads

Tier 3 can help find leads, but should not be the only support for major claims.

## Freshness rules

Fresh web verification is required for:

- recent artworks/campaigns/events
- legal status
- market prices and auction values
- identity claims
- business ownership/acquisitions
- current rankings or “best” lists
- live tool/platform details

## Forbidden ingestion

Do not ingest:

- full copyrighted books/articles
- leaked private documents
- doxxing material
- illegal instructions
- private personal data
- API keys or secrets
- scraped paywalled content beyond fair-use notes

## Required source notes

Every important source should capture:

- title
- publisher / author
- URL
- date published or last accessed
- source tier
- what it proves
- confidence
- caveats

## SPARKY Wiki safety boundary

Use this material for analysis, source governance, lawful adaptation, risk/ethics/consequence mapping, and disputed/needs-source labelling. Do not treat it as an instruction to run autonomous agents, browse the web, call APIs, change provider routing, override current app truth, or execute unlawful/deceptive activity.
