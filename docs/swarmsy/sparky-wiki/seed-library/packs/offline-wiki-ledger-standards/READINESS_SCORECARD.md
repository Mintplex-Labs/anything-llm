# Knowledge Pack Readiness Scorecard

A subject pack has a maximum score of 100.

| Area                             | Points | What good looks like                                              |
| -------------------------------- | -----: | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Source coverage                  |     20 | primary sources, reputable secondary sources, dates, source notes |
| Factual baseline                 |     10 | clean overview, key facts, no obvious gaps                        |
| Timeline                         |     10 | chronological anchors and major events                            |
| Key works/campaigns/examples     |     10 | enough examples to answer practical questions                     |
| Pattern / strategy analysis      |     15 | why it worked, audience psychology, distribution, positioning     |
| SPARKY Wiki reuse mechanics      |     10 | lawful, repeatable, app-relevant frameworks                       |
| Safety / legal / ethics boundary |     10 | risks and forbidden outputs are explicit                          |
| Source cards / provenance        |     10 | claims trace to sources, no blind dumps                           |
| Sparky summary / retrieval fit   |      5 | short answer, tags, aliases, canonical questions                  |

## Readiness bands

### 85–100: Ready

The app can answer most user questions locally. Live AI is optional unless the question is recent/current.

### 70–84: Usable

The app can answer basic questions locally. Sparky should mention that deeper or current verification may require live AI.

### 50–69: Starter

The pack is useful for indexing but should not be treated as complete.

### 0–49: Not ready

The pack should not power confident user-facing answers.

## Canonical question test

Each ready pack should answer at least 25 canonical user questions locally. Examples:

- Who/what is this subject?
- Why does it matter?
- What are the major timeline events?
- What are the major works/campaigns/examples?
- What made the strategy work?
- What failed or caused backlash?
- What can SPARKY Wiki reuse legally?
- What should Sparky avoid saying?
- What claims need fresh web verification?
