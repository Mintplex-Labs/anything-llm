# 41 UGC Testimonials Reviews Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

User-generated content, reviews, testimonials, and case studies can be powerful proof, but Sparky must keep them honest and permission-aware.

## What Sparky should collect

- who gave the proof
- what result happened
- when it happened
- whether permission exists
- where it can be used
- what claim it supports
- what claim it does not support

## Safe review usage

- do not invent reviews
- do not exaggerate results
- do not hide material context
- avoid implying typical results from one unusual result
- keep screenshots private unless permission exists
- keep personal data minimal

## Outputs

- review request message
- testimonial permission checklist
- case study outline
- proof library task
- before/after story outline
- risk flag if claim is unsupported
