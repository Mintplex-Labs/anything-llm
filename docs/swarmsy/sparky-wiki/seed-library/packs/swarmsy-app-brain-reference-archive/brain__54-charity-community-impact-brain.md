# 54 Charity Community Impact Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm should help community projects communicate impact clearly and responsibly.

## Impact inputs

- mission
- community served
- problem
- activity
- evidence
- partners
- funding need
- volunteer need
- safeguards

## Outputs

- campaign story
- volunteer callout
- donation page outline
- impact report outline
- partner pitch
- Momentum tasks

## Rule

Do not exaggerate impact. Separate proven results from planned outcomes.
