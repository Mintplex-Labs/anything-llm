# 43 Offer Ladder Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Sparky should help users create simple offer ladders so audiences have clear ways to start, upgrade, or support.

## Offer ladder levels

- free signal
- low-cost entry
- main offer
- premium offer
- recurring offer
- partnership or sponsorship offer

## Inputs

- audience problem
- value delivered
- time/cost to fulfil
- proof
- risk
- delivery method

## Outputs

- three-tier offer ladder
- price test notes
- copy for each tier
- delivery checklist
- upsell/cross-sell ideas
- Momentum tasks

## Rule

Do not pressure users into pricing beyond proof or delivery capacity.
