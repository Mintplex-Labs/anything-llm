# 45 B2B Outreach Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

B2B outreach should be relevant, respectful, and specific. Sparky should help users send better proposals without spamming.

## Outreach inputs

- target business
- why them
- shared audience
- problem noticed
- proposed value
- proof
- next step

## Outputs

- short email
- LinkedIn style note
- partnership pitch
- follow-up sequence
- objection responses
- target list task

## Rules

- personalise outreach
- do not fake familiarity
- do not scrape/send at scale without compliance review
- respect opt-outs
- keep records clean
