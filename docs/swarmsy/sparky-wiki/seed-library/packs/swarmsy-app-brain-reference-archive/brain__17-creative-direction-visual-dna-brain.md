# 17 Creative Direction Visual DNA Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Visual DNA is the look, mood, colour, layout, asset style, and recognisable signal of a brand or campaign.

## Outputs

- sticker brief
- social tile brief
- QR visual brief
