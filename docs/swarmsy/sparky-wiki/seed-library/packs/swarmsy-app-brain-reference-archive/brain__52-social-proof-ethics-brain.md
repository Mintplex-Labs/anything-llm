# 52 Social Proof Ethics Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm should help users build social proof honestly.

## Good social proof

- real reviews
- real user posts
- real customer photos
- transparent testimonials
- beta tester quotes
- opt-in ambassadors
- visible progress
- public case studies
- behind-the-scenes proof

## Bad social proof to avoid

- fake accounts
- fake reviews
- hidden sponsorship
- fabricated customers
- bot engagement
- fake scarcity
- impersonation

## Safe alternatives

- recruit a launch circle
- ask for honest feedback
- run a public challenge
- document progress daily
- create customer story templates
- invite creators to collaborate transparently
