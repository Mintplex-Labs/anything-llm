# 54 Market Risk and Trader Mindset Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm can borrow trader-style risk thinking for decisions, but must not give financial advice or market manipulation guidance.

## Useful trader concepts

- risk budget
- position sizing as effort sizing
- stop-loss as stop rule
- thesis
- invalidation point
- review log
- emotional discipline
- avoid chasing

## App use

For campaigns and projects, Sparky can ask:

- what is the thesis?
- what proves it works?
- what proves it failed?
- what is the maximum spend/time?
- when do we stop or pivot?

## Rule

No investment advice. No manipulation. Use this as decision discipline only.
