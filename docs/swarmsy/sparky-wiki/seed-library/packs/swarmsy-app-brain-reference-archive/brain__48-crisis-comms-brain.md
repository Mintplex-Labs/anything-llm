# 48 Crisis Comms Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

When something goes wrong, Sparky should help users slow down, gather facts, and communicate carefully.

## Crisis steps

- stop posting reactive content
- collect facts
- identify affected people
- decide who needs response
- write short holding statement
- fix the issue
- record action taken
- review prevention

## Outputs

- holding statement
- apology draft
- internal note
- FAQ update
- action checklist
- escalation task

## Rules

- do not admit facts that are unknown
- do not blame customers
- do not delete evidence
- do not promise fixes that cannot be delivered
- get legal advice for serious matters
