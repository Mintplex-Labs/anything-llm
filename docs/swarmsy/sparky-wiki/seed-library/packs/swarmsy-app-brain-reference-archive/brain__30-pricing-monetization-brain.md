# 30 Pricing and Monetization Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm should help users price offers and monetise attention without hiding costs or making unrealistic claims.

Inputs:
- cost to produce
- time required
- delivery cost
- target margin
- audience budget
- competitor range
- perceived value
- repeat purchase chance

Outputs:
- price range
- offer ladder
- break-even point
- risk note
- test plan
- Momentum task
