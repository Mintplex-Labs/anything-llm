# 08 Public Promotion Safety Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm can plan public-facing promotion, but outputs must stay lawful, permission-based, temporary, ethical, and brand-safe.

## Safe options

- shop window display
- venue noticeboard
- legal wall project
- community board
- market stall sign
- QR flyer with permission
- partner venue poster
- website landing page
- social post

## Rule

No trespass, property damage, harassment, or unsafe crowd tactics.
