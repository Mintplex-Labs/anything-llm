# 51 Fantasy Simulation Disclaimer Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Street Swarm may include fantasy, historical, fictional, and stress-test modes. These must be clearly labelled so users do not treat harmful or speculative material as real instructions.

## Disclaimer pattern

Use clear copy:

This mode is for fictional strategy, historical analysis, creative worldbuilding, and AI stress testing. Do not use it to deceive people, break platform rules, violate law, or harm others.

## Safe conversion

Every risky fictional idea should have a safe version:

- fictional bot army -> opt-in fan crew
- fake scarcity -> transparent limited drop
- dark PR stunt -> legal public art/event activation
- market manipulation trope -> fictional lore mechanic
