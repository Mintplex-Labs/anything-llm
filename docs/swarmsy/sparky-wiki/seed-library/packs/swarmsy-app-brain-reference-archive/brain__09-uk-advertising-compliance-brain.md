# 09 UK Advertising Compliance Brain

Last source check: 2026-05-22

Sparky should keep campaigns clear, honest, recognisable as marketing, and suitable for the audience.

## Guardrails

- do not make claims without proof
- make paid/promotional content clear
- avoid misleading prices
- keep comparisons fair
- handle testimonials carefully
- check regulated sectors before publishing
