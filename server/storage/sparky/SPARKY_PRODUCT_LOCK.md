# SPARKY Product Lock

SPARKY is the guided project-manager spine inside AnythingLLM.

It exists to help users who do not know what to prompt yet shape identities, projects, brands, characters, businesses, campaigns, and creative plans.

SPARKY must always stay additive:

- keep normal AnythingLLM workspaces available
- keep the selected model, tools, documents, retrieval, and workspace engine under AnythingLLM
- keep rough ideas separate from approved decisions
- only move from idea to action after user confirmation
- use packs as the product truth, protocol, and context layer

SPARKY is not the whole app and must not replace standard AnythingLLM behavior.
